The appendix entry, and why it is the day's real signal

Infosys is out of scope as a subject - an IT services firm selling AI implementation is a shovel vendor, and its AI revenue is its own sales, not a corporate's deployment ROI. It goes to the appendix and is never counted. But it is the sharpest disclosure on the tape, for a reason that inverts the usual reading.

The metric is unusually honest by construction. Per Dataquest's deep dive of the call, 8.2% covers six named value pools and excludes conventional engagements with AI tools bolted on - the exclusion that turns most AI-revenue metrics into the denominator games METHOD rejects. On that tighter definition AI revenue went from ~5.5% to 8.2% in two quarters.

And in the same release the company cut its growth guidance. Management named the mechanism - client pressure to deliver existing work with "fewer people, fewer hours or lower costs", reported as "AI-led deflation" - and conceded the two effects "cannot always be separated cleanly" because clients bundle productivity concessions into the same contracts that award new AI work. HCLTech is cited in the same piece estimating portfolio deflation at 2-3%; not logged, second-hand and undated.

Gross AI revenue up ~50% in two quarters; net top line guided down. If the firm whose entire business model is selling AI cannot show AI as net additive to its own top line, the prior on non-tech incumbents showing it should fall, not rise. That is a harder constraint on the S2 story than any of the null surveys already in this log, because it comes from the party with the strongest incentive to report the opposite.

Infosys IT services · India S2 — Share⚠ Counter-evidence

In the Q1 FY27 results release (23 Jul 2026) Infosys disclosed "AI as a % of Revenue" of 8.2% on total revenue of $5,082m (derived: approx. $417m), up from approximately 5.5% two quarters earlier. CEO Salil Parekh: "AI momentum is now rapidly converting into revenue, which demonstrates how Infosys' differentiated enterprise AI value proposition is translating into consistent market share gains." In the same release Infosys narrowed FY27 constant-currency revenue growth guidance to 1.5%-3.0% from 1.5%-3.5%, with reported growth of 2.4% YoY in constant currency.

Mechanism
Infosys sells AI-first services across six named value pools - AI strategy, process redesign, agents, data modernisation, AI engineering, platform-led implementation. Per Dataquest's account of the call, the metric deliberately EXCLUDES conventional engagements that merely have AI tools bolted onto existing workflows; those are tracked internally and not disclosed. That is a materially tighter definition than the 'AI-influenced revenue' constructs METHOD rejects, and it is the reason this is loggable at all. Running the other way, management described client pressure to use coding assistants and agents to complete existing work with fewer people and hours - reported as 'AI-led deflation' - compressing application maintenance, infrastructure operations and software development revenue, and said the two effects 'cannot always be separated cleanly' because clients bundle productivity concessions into contracts that also award new AI work.
Why this shape
S2, and the CEO says so himself: 'market share gains'. Absent Infosys' AI capability these buyers would have bought transformation and engineering services from Accenture, TCS, Wipro, Cognizant or Capgemini - test (a) fails for S3, a competitor lost the sale. Test (c) is arguably passed (agentic delivery is a genuine post-2024 capability) but capability novelty alone does not make new demand. Zero-sum at industry level; GDP-neutral. Subject is out of scope for the main log in METHOD's own terms - an IT services firm selling AI implementation is a shovel vendor, and its AI revenue describes its own sales rather than a corporate's deployment ROI - so it is routed to the appendix and never counted. Flagged counter_evidence because the pairing is the finding: AI revenue share rose roughly 50% in two quarters and the company cut total growth guidance in the same release. Gross AI revenue up, net top line down. This is the sharpest quantification yet found of AI revenue substituting for the base rather than adding to it - and it comes from a firm whose whole business is selling AI, which is the population most incentivised to show the opposite.

American International Group (AIG) Insurance · US S1 — Velocity

On the Q1 2026 earnings call (1 May 2026) Chairman and CEO Peter Zaffino said: "AIG Assist has helped deliver a 30% improvement on quoting more submissions, reduced time to quote for the underwriters by 55% and increased binding of submissions by approximately 40%." Zaffino also said that in 2025 AIG "launched Underwriting by AIG Assist to help our underwriters review our submissions with more and higher-quality information in a fraction of the time" and that after launch AIG "began to deploy AIG Assist across 8 lines of business." On the Q2 2026 call (7 Aug 2026) the same programme was described in words only - "Where deployed, our underwriters are reviewing more submissions and generating quotes significantly faster" - with no figures repeated and no premium, new-business or hit-ratio conversion attached.

Mechanism
AIG Assist is a multi-agent system with an orchestration layer that ingests and structures broker submissions, first in Lexington middle-market property (E&S) and then across eight lines of business. It does not price risk; it removes the clerical bottleneck between a submission arriving and an underwriter being able to quote it. The revenue path is pure capacity arithmetic: more submissions quoted per underwriter-hour, multiplied by a higher submit-to-bind ratio, equals more bound premium - provided the extra business is not adversely selected and the hit rate holds.
Why this shape
S1. All three disclosed numbers are input metrics - submission throughput, cycle time, and submit-to-bind conversion. No premium, revenue or ratio figure is attached to AIG Assist anywhere in the disclosure. Revenue conversion is conspicuously absent, and the absence hardened between quarters: the figures were given in Q1 2026 and were not repeated on the Q2 2026 call, which reverted to qualitative language. Logged today primarily as a correction to this log's own 2026-08-29 entry, which reported via FactSet that AIG "attributed no ratio move" and that the only AI-attached number across seven P&C carriers' Q2 calls was Travelers' 0.5-point loss-ratio move. That is accurate about the Q2 calls FactSet reviewed and misleading as a characterisation of the sector: the largest carrier in the set had put three discrete AI-attributed operating numbers on the record one quarter earlier. The disclosure is four months old and is logged for that corrective reason under METHOD's follow-up discipline, not as fresh news.

Kohl's Corporation Retail · US S1 — Velocity⚠ Counter-evidence

On the Q2 2026 earnings call (26 Aug 2026) CEO Michael Bender said: "We're also encouraged by the early signals from agentic commerce. Adoption is still small, but customers who engage with our AI shopping assistant are showing stronger conversion and higher revenue per visit." No conversion figure, no revenue-per-visit figure, no basket or attach number was given - in prepared remarks or in Q&A. The surrounding disclosed numbers are non-AI: digital sales up 2.8%, marketplace GMV up 88%, comparable sales down 0.9%.

Mechanism
Kohl's launched a Google Gemini-powered shopping assistant for the 2026 back-to-school season, on a rebuilt digital platform. The claimed mechanism is the same one Walmart, Target and Lowe's have already put numbers against in this log: conversational product discovery raising conversion and order value on the digital channel.
Why this shape
S1 tagged as counter-evidence. Named company, Tier A source, dated, specific and identifiable mechanism - but no number, and the CEO volunteers the smallness of adoption in the same sentence as the benefit. It is logged because the non-quantification is the finding, not despite it. Every comparable deployment already in this log arrived with a figure attached: Walmart's Sparky users spending 40% more per order, Target's near-20% conversion lift on back-to-school pages, Lowe's Mylow engagement conversion. Kohl's shipped the same class of assistant, on the same seasonal calendar, against the same vendor stack, and reported direction without magnitude. That is the base rate the quantified disclosures should be read against, and it is invisible if only the quantified ones get logged.

Etsy Retail · US S2 — Share

On the Q2 2026 earnings call (6 Aug 2026) CEO Kruti Patel Goyal said `"traffic from agentic experiences is still less than 1% of our overall traffic"`, and CFO Lanny Baker characterised it as `"higher intent, higher average order value traffic"`. Etsy also launched Gifting Assistant, a conversational AI tool, in beta. No dollar attribution and no isolated conversion multiple. Etsy marketplace GMS `$2.6b`, +7.5% YoY. Logged with age noted: disclosure is 14 days old, missed by prior runs.

Mechanism
Etsy is (a) building conversational discovery on-site (Gifting Assistant) and (b) integrating with third-party LLM/agent platforms so that Etsy listings surface in agent-driven shopping flows. Claimed revenue mechanism is incremental orders from agent-referred sessions that would otherwise have converted at a competitor.
Why this shape
S2: absent Etsy's presence in AI agent flows, buyers who used an LLM or shopping agent to find a gift would have bought a similar item from Amazon, Target, or a smaller marketplace - a competitor loses the sale. Zero-sum at industry level, GDP-neutral. Independent second confirmation of the pattern Booking's CFO named on 4 Aug 2026 (already logged S2) - AI-driven traffic remains a sub-1% share but converts and monetises at above-average rates. No counterfactual purchase test for S3 is even attempted.

Lowe's Companies Retail · US S1 — Velocity

On the Q2 fiscal 2026 earnings call (19 Aug 2026) CEO Marvin Ellison said Lowe's MyLow AI shopping agent has fielded roughly `25 million` customer and associate questions since launch, and that `"customers who engage with MyLow while browsing online convert at three times the rate of those who don't"`. Digital sales grew `15.7%` YoY in Q2 - second consecutive quarter above 15% - but Lowe's did not attribute any share of digital growth, or any dollar figure, specifically to MyLow. Total comps `+0.2%`, total sales `$26.0b`.

Mechanism
MyLow is a customer-facing conversational agent that answers project and product questions inside the online shopping flow, and a linked MyLow Companion tool that puts real-time product knowledge in the hands of ~300k in-store associates. The revenue mechanism claimed is a conversion-rate lift on the digital funnel; the in-store version is pitched at attach and re-shop but not sized.
Why this shape
S1: the AI-attributed number is a conversion multiple - an input metric - not revenue. Revenue conversion is conspicuously absent rather than merely unstated: Ellison and CFO Brandon Sink disclosed digital sales +15.7% and did not break out any share of that (or of Pro or Online) as MyLow-attributed. The '3x' compares a self-selected cohort (customers who chose to open MyLow) to non-users on the same site - the standard selection-bias issue for any voluntary AI feature. Same shape as the Zillow AI-mode 3x claim (5 Aug 2026, already in the log) and the Target back-to-school +20% conversion claim (19 Aug 2026).

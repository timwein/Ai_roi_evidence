Nano-X Imaging Ltd. Healthcare / medical imaging · Israel S3 candidate (unproven)⚠ Counter-evidence

In its H1 2026 operating and financial review, furnished to the SEC as Exhibit 99.3 to a Form 6-K on 9 Sep 2026, Nano-X disclosed segment revenue of $2,083 thousand for AI and Software Solutions in the six months to 30 June 2026 against $273 thousand in the comparable period - stated in the filing as "an increase in revenue from AI and Software Solutions during the Reported Period was $1.7 million, or 663%." The same paragraph states the increase was "primarily due to the consolidation of the Nanox Health IT business, which accounted for $1.8 million of revenue in the Reported Period." Backing the acquired business out leaves roughly $0.3 million of AI and software revenue against $0.273 million a year earlier. Total company revenue was $8.467 million, up 45%, of which teleradiology services were $6.055 million and Nanox.ARC imaging systems $329 thousand. Logged as counter-evidence: the headline 663% AI growth rate is consolidation of an acquired business, not demand for AI.

Mechanism
Nanox.AI sells algorithms that re-read CT scans already taken for some other clinical reason and flag incidental findings - coronary artery calcium, vertebral compression fractures, fatty liver. The asserted new-TAM mechanism is opportunistic screening: revenue from a population nobody was screening, because no one was going to pay a radiologist to re-read a scan looking for something the referring physician did not order. Health systems pay per study analysed, and downstream they capture the follow-on care the finding generates.
Why this shape
Filed as S3-CANDIDATE and as counter-evidence to S3, not as a confirmed S3. (a) Counterfactual purchase: plausibly yes - nobody was buying a second read of an already-acquired scan - but the filing establishes none of it. (b) Prior alternative: the prior alternative was doing nothing, which is the S3 shape. (c) Capability gate: weak. Automated CT quantification of calcium scores is pre-2024 machine vision, not a frontier capability. (d) Newly economic: unproven, and this filing is the evidence against it - stripped of the Nanox Health IT consolidation the AI line is roughly $0.3m on a half-year, essentially flat year over year, which is what an unserved market that stays unserved looks like. Scope is appendix because the subject sells the AI rather than deploying it; the deploying corporations are the health systems, and none of them are named or quantified here.

Research Solutions, Inc. Information services / research workflow software · US S2 — Share

In its FY2026 results release, filed as an SEC Form 8-K exhibit on 9 Sep 2026 for the year ended 30 June 2026, Research Solutions disclosed "Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) up 7.8% to $22.5 million, which includes approximately $16.2 million of B2B recurring revenue and $6.3 million of B2C recurring revenue... This included AI related ARR of $0.8 million." The Q4 bullet states the same $0.8 million "grew 125% sequentially from the third quarter of fiscal 2026." Total revenue was $48.3 million, down 1.5% year over year, with transaction revenue falling to $27.5 million from $30.1 million. The named AI products are Scite MCP and Article Galaxy MCP, which the company says "allow researchers to utilize the unique functionality of Scite and Article Galaxy inside ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, and any AI tools researchers already use."

Mechanism
MCP connectors expose the company's citation-analysis and document-delivery back end inside the LLM assistants researchers are already working in, so the platform is billed as a tool call rather than as a destination web app. Revenue is recurring subscription ARR from university libraries and life-science R&D groups.
Why this shape
S2, not S3, and the distinction is the point. Test (b) fails for S3: the buyers are existing academic and life-science customers who were already paying for Article Galaxy and Scite, so this is a new access channel priced to the installed base, not demand that did not previously exist. Test (c) passes cleanly - an MCP server addressing ChatGPT and Claude could not have been built 24 months ago - which is exactly why capability novelty alone must never be allowed to carry an S3 call. Scope is appendix because the subject is a software vendor selling AI, not a non-tech corporate deploying it. The scale is the finding: $0.8m is 3.6% of ARR and 1.7% of revenue at a company that describes itself as "the leading AI-powered research workflow platform," and total revenue still fell 1.5%.

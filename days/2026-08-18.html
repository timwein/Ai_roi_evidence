Customers Bancorp Banking · US S1 — Velocity

In its Q2 2026 results release (23 Jul 2026) CEO Sam Sidhu said the bank "also saw positive revenue impact, with select verticals delivering over 100% improvement in prospecting success rates as AI enhanced our ability to identify and pursue the highest-quality opportunities", and disclosed a completed pilot of an AI-powered loan closing process that closed selected commercial loans "in seven days, down from 30 to 60 days typically".

Mechanism
Two distinct revenue-producing processes. (1) Commercial prospecting: AI ranks and targets prospects inside named verticals, raising the share of pursued opportunities that convert to a mandate - a hit-rate effect, not a volume effect. (2) Commercial loan closing: an AI-assisted documentation and closing workflow compresses time-to-close from 30-60 days to 7 in pilot, which frees origination capacity and shortens the interval between commitment and interest-earning asset.
Why this shape
S1, not S2. Both disclosed numbers are input metrics - a prospecting hit rate and a cycle time - and neither is a revenue figure. The revenue conversion is exactly the interesting absence: Sidhu explicitly asserts "positive revenue impact" and then puts no dollar, no basis-point spread and no loan-growth decomposition next to it, in a quarter where loans grew 16.9% and deposits 14.5% for reasons the release attributes to franchise build-out generally. A CEO who is willing to say the words "positive revenue impact" in a Tier A filing and unwilling to size them is the standing pattern of this log, stated unusually plainly.

BHP Group Mining · Australia C4 — Non-tech · non-engineering

BHP's FY26 results (17 Aug 2026) disclose US$470 million of EBITDA from technology initiatives in FY26 - US$330 million delivered in-year plus a US$140 million exit run-rate - against a target exit run-rate beyond US$650 million in FY27; on the call CEO Brandon Craig said that "by the end of 2026, technology initiatives were delivering incremental EBITDA at an annual run rate of almost $500 million". Autonomous haulage is now roughly 50% of the truck fleet.

Mechanism
Named AI applications sit inside a wider technology programme: AI-led leaching optimisation at Escondida lifting copper recoveries, predictive and preventative maintenance powered by AI to cut unplanned downtime, autonomous haulage across about half the truck fleet, and pit-to-port supply-chain optimisation. The disclosed EBITDA is the programme's output, not any one of these.
Why this shape
C4 - non-tech sector, non-engineering function - and a textbook instance of standing cost trap 3, composite value metrics. The number is attributed to "technology initiatives", not to AI; AI is named as a component and never sized, so no AI-attributed figure can be extracted. It is also not cleanly a cost saving: improved copper recoveries raise saleable output, which is a volume effect, so the composite mixes cost avoidance with production gain. Logged once, in the cost ledger, per the single-ledger rule; the revenue reading is noted here rather than double-logged. The two figures do not obviously reconcile - US$470m FY26 total versus a CEO run rate of "almost $500 million" - which is itself evidence the construct is loosely defined.

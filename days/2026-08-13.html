Allstate Insurance (P&C) · US S2 — Share⚠ Counter-evidence

On the Q2 2026 call (5 Aug 2026) CEO Tom Wilson introduced ALLIE - Allstate's Large Language Intelligent Ecosystem - an eight-component agentic AI platform that "will leverage Agentic AI to improve customer value, lower cost and increase growth", built on "250+ analytical models", "40+ petabytes" of data and "1.5 billion CPU compute hours". Asked to size it, Wilson said: "I can't give you specific numbers how much growth is going to do or how we can do at expenses." The platform is "not completely built or deployed". Issued applications rose 9.9% and net premiums written 2.6% in the quarter; neither was attributed to AI.

Mechanism
ALLIE is an agent-to-agent orchestration layer over Allstate's existing model estate, intended to touch customer engagement, direct policy sales in three states, pricing accuracy and claims. The growth mechanism management gestures at is conversion and pricing precision at the quote - Allstate generates over 100 million quotes and purchases roughly 50 million leads a year, so small conversion movements are large absolute numbers. None of that is a disclosed result; it is the architecture of one.
Why this shape
Counter-evidence, tagged S2 because incremental premium won on existing auto and home products from existing consumers is the shape Allstate is implicitly claiming, and it is zero-sum - every policy Allstate writes at a better price is one Progressive, GEICO or State Farm did not write. The finding is the refusal: the CEO of a top-tier US insurer announced a named enterprise AI platform, listed compute and model counts to four significant figures, and would not put a number on either the expense or the growth benefit. The quantified metrics offered - 250 models, 40 petabytes, 1.5 billion CPU hours - are pure adoption and scale, METHOD's standing trap 1. Note the ordering in Wilson's own sentence: customer value, lower cost, then growth. Growth is third and the least specified. The 9.9% issued-application growth in the same quarter is credited to a "multifaceted approach" of affordability, product, distribution, marketing and claims - not to AI - which is attribution discipline by the company, and worth recording as such.

Cars.com Auto retail / Digital marketplace · US S1 — Velocity

On the Q2 2026 call (6 Aug 2026) CEO Tobias Hartmann said "around 20% of active searches on Cars.com are engaging with Carson", that "Carson users are 4x more likely to submit a lead", and that Carson users "accounted for nearly 30% of total leads submitted in the month of June". Total Q2 revenue rose 1% to $179.9m; marketplace revenue rose more than 7% while OEM and national advertising fell 18%. No revenue was attributed to Carson.

Mechanism
Carson is an LLM shopping assistant inside the Cars.com marketplace. Cars.com does not sell cars; it sells lead flow and subscription packages to franchise and independent dealers. So the revenue path runs assistant engagement -> lead volume and lead quality -> dealer willingness to pay for marketplace packages (Premium Plus, the fastest-growing package in the quarter, targeted at 15% adoption by year-end). Carson sits upstream of the thing that is actually monetized, which is why the disclosed numbers are lead metrics rather than dollars.
Why this shape
S1. Every disclosed figure is an input metric - engagement rate, lead-submission propensity, share of leads - and none is revenue. Revenue conversion was NOT disclosed, and the surrounding print argues against assuming it: total revenue grew 1% in the same quarter that AI-assisted leads reportedly reached ~30% of the June total. Either the lead-quality gain has not yet repriced dealer packages, or the 4x figure is largely selection - shoppers already close to submitting a lead are the ones who engage an assistant. Cars.com offers no control group, exactly as Amazon offered none for "spend 40% more per order" (logged 12 Aug). Note also that ~30% of leads is a June figure, not a Q2 figure; the quarter-level number was not given.

Xometry Industrials / Manufacturing marketplace · US S1 — Velocity

On the Q2 2026 call (4 Aug 2026) CEO Sanjeev Sahni said "our AI models are effectively optimizing conversion, buyer growth and market share gains", disclosing an "approximately 15% improvement in CNC cost prediction accuracy" from a new-generation model and process-recommendation acceptance reaching "85%, driven by an upgraded context-aware AI process recommender". Marketplace revenue grew 45% YoY to $215.4m, total revenue 41% to a record $229.3m, active buyers 20% to 89,557; marketing spend fell to a record-low 3.4% of marketplace revenue.

Mechanism
Xometry quotes custom-manufactured parts instantly from an uploaded CAD file and routes the job to a supplier network, earning the spread. Pricing accuracy is therefore the P&L: underprice and gross margin is lost on the job, overprice and the quote does not convert. A 15% improvement in cost-prediction accuracy widens the band of jobs that can be quoted profitably, and the process recommender picking the right manufacturing process 85% of the time removes a human sourcing step. Sahni's framing that the pricing, sourcing and manufacturability models are "no longer independently doing" their jobs but "all interconnected" is the specific 2025-26 change.
Why this shape
S1. The disclosed AI numbers are input metrics - prediction accuracy and recommendation acceptance - not revenue. The 45% marketplace growth is real and management links it verbally to the models, but no dollar of it is attributed, and buyer growth of 20% against revenue growth of 45% means most of the delta is wallet share and mix, which management did not decompose. Revenue conversion is therefore asserted, not disclosed. Explicitly NOT S3: these are better quotes for parts buyers who already buy machined parts from job shops, so absent Xometry the demand exists and someone serves it - test (a) fails. Scope call: kept in the main log rather than the appendix because Xometry's revenue is the sale of manufactured parts, not software licences; it is a distributor with an ML pricing engine, not a vendor selling AI. Reasonable people could push it to the appendix, and the ML quoting engine long predates the 24-month capability frontier.

Shopify E-commerce platform (out of scope - software) · CA S2 — Share

On the Q2 2026 call (5 Aug 2026) President Harley Finkelstein said "AI-driven traffic and also orders to Shopify stores tripled year-over-year in the second quarter", that new buyer orders from AI channels "are coming in at nearly twice the rate of other channels", that "AI searches powered by Catalog converted twice the rate of those using scraped data", and that "75% of AI-referred orders originated from outside the top 100 merchant categories". GMV grew 32% to $115.6bn and revenue 34% to $3.6bn. No dollar amount of revenue was attributed to AI; management called AI "small relative to our massive GMV".

Mechanism
LLM assistants answer a shopping intent in natural language and resolve it against structured product data. Shopify's Catalog exposes its merchants' inventory to those assistants in machine-readable form rather than leaving them to scrape it, and the 2x conversion gap between Catalog-sourced and scraped results is the price of that structure. Because assistants match intent rather than keywords, the products they surface skew away from the categories that win search-engine auctions.
Why this shape
Appendix: Shopify is a software platform and out of scope as a subject in the main revenue log. Classified S2, not S3, and the distinction is the whole point of retrieving this. The long-tail statistic - 75% of AI-referred orders outside the top 100 categories - is the strongest S3-shaped claim retrieved this run, and it still fails the tests. (a) Counterfactual purchase: not established; a buyer who finds a niche product through an assistant was in most cases going to buy something in that need-state from somebody. (b) Prior alternative: the prior alternative was keyword search, which resolved to head merchants and paid placement - so this reallocates demand from head to tail, and from Google-mediated discovery to assistant-mediated discovery. Someone lost those orders. (c) Capability gate: passes cleanly - intent-resolving assistants at consumer scale did not exist 24 months ago. (d) Newly economic: partially - discovery cost for long-tail SKUs collapsed - but Shopify discloses no evidence that total category demand rose rather than moved. Three of four tests are not met, so this is a distribution shift, not new TAM. Also note what is absent: after tripling, still no dollar figure, and an explicit management caveat that the base is small.

Ford Motor Company Automotive · US C3 — Non-tech · engineering⚠ Counter-evidence

Ford VP of vehicle hardware engineering Charles Poon said the company "mistakenly" believed that introducing AI and adjusting design requirements would produce a high-quality product, and that Ford has since rehired, newly hired or promoted experienced engineers into vehicle engineering to fix the resulting quality gaps - reported as approximately 300 by Fox Business (citing a Bloomberg press call) and as 350 by The Next Web (citing The Verge). Poon: "Artificial intelligence is a fantastic tool, but it's only as good as the information you use to train it." Ford subsequently topped J.D. Power's 2026 US Initial Quality Study at 152 problems per 100 vehicles.

Mechanism
Ford substituted AI-assisted design-requirement tooling for experienced engineering judgement in vehicle quality. The failure mode Poon describes is not that the models were bad but that the veterans who held the tacit knowledge left before it was captured, so the tools amplified weak inputs instead of catching design flaws. The correction was headcount - re-hiring senior engineers - plus, per the reporting, a large expansion of AI-powered edge-case testing running alongside the humans rather than instead of them.
Why this shape
C3 - non-tech company, engineering function. Counter-evidence to the cost thesis in its strongest available form: not a study, not a survey, but a named incumbent that made the substitution, measured the result, and bought the labour back. It bears on METHOD's standing trap 4 in reverse - here the headcount action has an explicitly AI-attributed cause, stated by the executive responsible, against the company's interest. Note carefully what it does and does not prove: Ford went on to lead J.D. Power initial quality, so the end state is AI plus experienced engineers beating both prior arrangements. The refutable claim is substitution, not augmentation.

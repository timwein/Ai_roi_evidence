Bank of America Corporation Banking · US C4 — Non-tech · non-engineering

At the Barclays 24th Annual Global Financial Services Conference on 14 Sep 2026, chairman and CEO Brian Moynihan put a dollar figure on Bank of America's AI programme for the first time: "We're now probably 130, 140 of implemented things for the cost of $400 million, generating a benefit of $800 million", adding "We expect to double the AI expense budget for it next year." He also said about 18,000 coders "use it and save 10, 15%" of time, that about 209,000 employees are expected to have access to AI tools, and that Erica has 20 million users and handled 200 million interactions in the last quarter. "Benefit" was not defined, no expense line was named as falling, and no revenue was attributed.

Mechanism
Roughly 130-140 implemented AI and machine-learning use cases out of more than 300 approved, spread across banker research and meeting preparation, developer coding assistance, fraud and control work, and the Erica assistant handling retail service contacts. Each case is individually approved on its own economics; the $800m is the bank's roll-up of those case-level estimates against $400m of programme cost. Erica is the volume lever - 200 million interactions in a quarter are contacts that do not reach a human channel.
Why this shape
C4: a bank, so non-tech, and the benefit pool is overwhelmingly non-engineering - retail service, banker preparation, fraud, operations. The 18,000-coder sub-claim is C3-shaped but METHOD's one-ledger rule puts the entry where the headline number sits. Standing trap 3 applies hard: "benefit" is an undefined composite that may mix cost avoidance, loss avoidance and revenue, and Moynihan did not say which. Trap 5 also applies - no realized expense reduction was claimed. This is a follow-up on the BofA entry of 30 Jul 2026, which recorded >20% developer productivity with no dollar attached; the dollar has now arrived, and it arrived alongside a decision to double the spend, which is what a management team does when it believes its own ratio.

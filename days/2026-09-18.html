Ford Motor Company Autos · US S2 — Share

Mike Aragon, President of Ford Integrated Services, gave the first full subscriber-and-ARPU disclosure for Ford's software services business at the Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference (8 Sep 2026) and repeated it at Morgan Stanley's 14th Annual Laguna Conference (17 Sep 2026): "530,000 total BlueCruise subscribers, up 40% year over year" against a "1.5 million" vehicle install base; "200,000" of those subscribe after the bundled trial ends, up 170% year over year; "1.6 million paying subscribers after vehicle purchase, excluding bundled trials" across the whole platform, including "900,000 Ford Pro commercial subscribers, up 20% year over year"; blended ARPU across Ford Pro and retail of "$14" a month; and on margins, "our margins are very healthy. They are very much in line with what you would expect in a SaaS business. Contribution margins are over 50%."

Mechanism
BlueCruise is a paid hands-free highway driving subscription sold on top of the vehicle. The AI is the perception-and-control stack; the revenue mechanism is the conversion of a bundled trial into a recurring paid subscription on an already-sold car, plus Ford Pro Intelligence telematics at $10 a month into commercial fleets. Revenue is subscriber count times price, at above-50% contribution margin, on hardware the customer already owns.
Why this shape
S2, not S3, and the distinction is the point. (a) Counterfactual purchase: a buyer who wants hands-free highway driving could buy GM Super Cruise or Tesla's driver-assist subscription instead - the demand pre-exists and is contested. (b) Prior alternative: paid competitor products, not nothing. (c) Capability gate: BlueCruise shipped in 2021, so it does not require any capability absent 24 months ago; this fails the gate cleanly. (d) Newly economic: no - the segment was already served. What is genuinely new is the disclosure quality, not the shape: a non-tech incumbent putting subscriber count, growth, ARPU and contribution margin on one slide is rare in this log. Note also this is a volume-and-price disclosure, not an attributed revenue figure - Ford did not give a dollar revenue line for Integrated Services at either event.

Salesforce, Inc. Software · US S2 — Share

In its Q2 FY2027 results (26 Aug 2026), restated to investors at Dreamforce on 16 Sep 2026, Salesforce disclosed that "Agentforce ARR exceeded $1.5 billion, up over 240% Y/Y" and that "Agentforce and Data 360 annual recurring revenue ("ARR") reached nearly $3.9 billion, up over 210% Y/Y", on total quarterly revenue of $11.3 billion. The same release carries the definition change that produces part of the growth rate: "Effective Q2 FY27, Agentforce ARR includes our AI offerings, Slackbot and Headless 360."

Mechanism
Agentforce is sold as consumption-priced AI agents on top of an existing Salesforce subscription. The revenue is the vendor's own sales of AI capability, not ROI on AI the vendor deployed in its own revenue-producing processes.
Why this shape
Appendix: the subject is a software vendor, out of scope for the revenue log under METHOD's 'shovels' exclusion, and is logged here only as a comparator for what incumbents will and will not quantify. S2 rather than S3: Agentforce ARR is displacing spend that buyers would otherwise put into competing automation, CRM add-ons or services headcount - the counterfactual purchase exists, so this reallocates rather than creates demand. The `240%` growth rate is not comparable to the prior year's because Salesforce moved the goalposts in the same quarter it reported: Slackbot and Headless 360 were folded into the Agentforce ARR definition effective Q2 FY27, and the release does not size the reclassification.

McKinsey State of AI 2026 global survey (1,719 respondents) Cross-sector · Global S2 — Share⚠ Counter-evidence

McKinsey's 2026 State of AI wave, reported 25 Aug 2026, finds 37% of respondents "attribute at least some EBIT impact to AI use" - "about the same" share as the 2025 survey - and only 6% qualify as "AI high performers" (at least 5% of EBIT attributed to AI plus a self-described "significant" impact), a figure that "remained flat since last year". 20% cited AI-related operating costs as constraining their use of the technology.

Mechanism
None - this entry measures the absence of a mechanism. The survey asks executives to attribute enterprise-level EBIT to AI use; the finding is that the share able to do so did not move over twelve months, and that among those who can, most put the figure below 5% of EBIT.
Why this shape
Counter-evidence tagged to S2, the shape that claims firm-level attributed financial impact on existing business. It contradicts the proposition that AI deployment is now producing bankable P&L effect at scale: on a 1,719-respondent base the attribution rate is flat year over year while capex is not. Logged despite METHOD's standing rejection of consultant surveys because the direction of the finding runs against the surveyor's commercial interest - selection bias in a McKinsey AI survey pushes toward overstating impact, so a null result is the one reading a consultant survey is well positioned to deliver. Not a revenue finding and not counted as one.

Royal Bank of Canada Banking · Canada C4 — Non-tech · non-engineering

On the Q3 2026 earnings call (27 Aug 2026) CEO Dave McKay said RBC is "accelerating the execution around our AI ambitions as we build towards generating CAD 700 million to CAD 1 billion in enterprise value by the end of fiscal 2027". RBC has previously defined that enterprise value as "the annualized exit run-rate benefit in revenue, cost avoidance, expense savings, fraud reduction and lower cost of risk, net of investments", incremental from fiscal 2024.

Mechanism
A dedicated AI Group reporting to the CEO, built on Borealis AI, an NVIDIA partnership and proprietary LLMs. Named workstreams on the call: advisor productivity tools in wealth management, back-office streamlining, and "AI for Alpha" across "30 plus customized investment processes". No workstream carries its own disclosed number.
Why this shape
Cost ledger, C4 - a non-tech incumbent, function is everything-but-engineering. It is not a revenue entry and must not be read as one: the target is a composite of five different things (revenue, cost avoidance, expense savings, fraud reduction, lower cost of risk) with no split published, which is METHOD's standing trap 3 exactly. RBC deserves credit for defining the composite at all - most issuers of a "value" number will not - but an undecomposed number cannot answer whether any of it is top line. The date-stamped, net-of-investment framing is the useful part: it is falsifiable, which almost no AI target is.

The pattern has changed shape this run, and not in the direction of the thesis. For five runs the finding was absence - non-tech incumbents disclosing funnel metrics and withholding the revenue line. This run the finding is retreat. The two largest US banks, on consecutive days, moved in the same direction: BofA restated a composite benefit roll-up down by roughly two thirds, and JPMorgan stopped producing one. Publicis Groupe Media / Advertising · FR S2 — Share

In its Third Quarter 2026 Revenue release (13 Oct 2026) Publicis reported Q3 net revenue of `EUR 3,808m`, up `7.9%` reported and `+5.0%` organic, accelerating from `+4.8%` in H1. Chairman and CEO Arthur Sadoun said: "Our marketing transformation capabilities benefited from the growing demand from clients for our AI-powered products and services", with Connected Media and Intelligent Creativity growing high-single-digit and single-digit respectively. The Connected Media practice review states the segment's performance "was driven by market share gains, rising demand for AI-powered products and services" and growth in new addressable markets. Connected Media is `62%` of group net revenue. No AI-specific revenue figure was given.

Mechanism
Publicis sells media planning, buying and creative services. AI-powered products raise the measurable performance of the campaigns it runs, which wins and retains client budgets in competitive pitches. The revenue is the client's media and creative spend, redirected to Publicis from a rival holding company.
Why this shape
S2, and Publicis says so itself: the stated driver is "market share gains". Who lost the sale - Omnicom/IPG and WPP, the other two holding companies in a consolidating three-horse market. Absent the AI, these clients would have bought media and creative services from somebody; the budget existed. Zero-sum at industry level and GDP-neutral. The release's "new addressable markets" phrase is the only S3-adjacent language and it is unsized, undefined and not tested against any of the four S3 criteria, so it is not promoted. Scope tension recorded: Publicis is a media and marketing-services group, in scope under METHOD's "media, professional services" - but its Technology arm, Publicis Sapient, is a digital consultancy, and the disclosure is framed as client demand for "our AI-powered products and services", which edges toward selling the shovels rather than deploying them. Logged main with that noted.

Citigroup Inc. Banking · US S1 — Velocity

On the Q3 2026 earnings call (13 Oct 2026) Chair and CEO Jane Fraser said "AI is accelerating our product development life cycle" and that "we are typically seeing about a 30-40% in increase in the speed with which we are launching products". She added that "around 100 of our most manual processes have or are being re engineered end to end leveraging AI and automation", that "14 thousand of our engineers use our main agentic coding tool", that "we have reduced our tech vendor spend by 30% year over year as a consequence", and that "this year alone, we are seeing the financial benefits from AI and automation significantly out[weigh] the investments" made year to date. On revenue she said only that "we are also using AI to generate revenue and improve the client experience" and that "it is helping us earn, and it is helping us save money" - with no revenue figure attached. Citi's Q3 revenue was `$23.8bn`, up `8%`, its best third quarter in a decade; none of it was attributed to AI.

Mechanism
Generative and agentic tooling compresses the build cycle for new banking products, so more SKUs reach market per unit of engineering capacity. Launch speed is the metric; the revenue it is supposed to produce arrives later, in products not yet named. The same tooling is credited with a 30% cut in third-party technology vendor spend as work moves in-house.
Why this shape
S1, not S2: the disclosed number is an input metric - the speed at which products are launched - not revenue. Revenue conversion was asserted but conspicuously unquantified. Fraser used the words "using AI to generate revenue" and "it is helping us earn" in the same breath as a precise `30-40%` velocity figure and a precise `30%` vendor-spend figure, which is the clearest single illustration this log has of the asymmetry it exists to measure: management had two cost-side numbers ready and no revenue-side number at all. The cost reading of this disclosure (vendor spend, the benefits-outweigh-investment claim) is noted here rather than double-logged as a C3/C4 entry, per METHOD's one-ledger rule; the entry is about launch velocity.

On the Q3 2026 call (14 Oct 2026) CEO Brian Moynihan said: "We are about 130 different projects implemented", "All of them have a payback, and they are very detailed projects", "Overall, the benefits are 2x-3x over time of the expense you are giving out", and - the number that matters - "We expect that number to go from the $250, $300 this year to $700 million next year". One month earlier, at the Barclays conference on 14 Sep 2026, the same CEO said of the same roughly `130-140` implemented projects: "for the cost of $400 million, generating a benefit of $800 million". The 2026 benefit has gone from `$800m` to `$250-300m` in thirty days with no stated restatement. He also said technology development is "$4 billion in change a year" where "we are 10% more efficient", and gave headcount as "down by 3,000, 1.5% year-over-year" with "managers down by about 5%" while "production teammates that are doing the client-facing work are actually up". No revenue was attributed to AI.

Mechanism
A roll-up: roughly 130 case-level AI projects across the bank, each with its own modelled payback, summed into a single 'benefit' figure that is never split into cost avoided and revenue gained. Composite 'value' metric, METHOD trap 3 - and now demonstrably unstable.
Why this shape
C4 (non-tech / non-engineering) for the bank-wide roll-up; the `$4bn` technology-development line with its `10%` efficiency gain is the C3 component and is noted here rather than double-logged. Counter-evidence because the restatement is the finding. The 2026-09-15 entry's falsifier read: 'if the benefit-to-cost ratio is never restated with a larger numerator on a later call, the $800m was a modelling exercise rolled up from case-level estimates, not money the bank kept.' It has now been restated with a numerator roughly a third the size, from the same speaker, one month later, on the higher-exposure venue of a quarterly earnings call rather than a conference fireside. Either the `$800m` was never a 2026 figure, or the definition changed silently.

Assessment

Nine findings and no S3. The week's signal is not a success story - it is that the four largest US retailers to report in the same seven days have converged on publishing an AI conversion multiple and refusing to publish a dollar.

Mid-November retail earnings delivered the densest cluster of AI conversion metrics this log has recorded. Walmart, Lowe's and Target each gave a multiple; none gave a number. Loblaw's CEO gave the same engagement story and then said out loud what the other three implied.

Sources read

  • Walmart - Q3 FY2027 call, 19 Nov. Furner: Sparky US usage +129% y/y, baskets +40%. Penetration nearly doubled from +70% in Q2 and the differential did not compress - the August falsifier resolved against the selection reading. Still no dollars, no share of e-commerce, no user count.
  • Lowe's - Q3 call, 18 Nov. Ellison repeated Mylow's 3x conversion unchanged and ">1m questions a month", and credited 17.4% online growth to Pro, DIY, traffic and fulfilment rather than to Mylow. Two quarters of an unsized multiple.
  • Target - Q3 call, 18 Nov. Fiddelke on Ask Target: guests "move to purchase at about twice the rate through that technology than we see elsewhere", product still in beta. First conversion figure for this SKU; comps +5.6% on 5% traffic.

  • TJX - Q3 call, 18 Nov. No AI content in any coverage retrieved.
Cross-run pattern, now hard to dismiss: the conversion multiple has become the standard non-tech AI disclosure, and it is never followed by a denominator. Lowe's 3x (two quarters), Walmart 40% (two quarters), Target 2x, Booking 2x, Kohl's, Costco, iQIYI 2x. Twelve weeks of these and not one company has converted a multiple into basis points of comp or a dollar of attributed sales.

The useful variation inside that pattern is which falsifier limb fires. Walmart's differential survived a near-doubling of penetration, which is the strongest causal evidence in the log and still not a revenue number. Lowe's repeated its multiple without converting it, which is the weaker outcome.

Ategrity is the week's most informative entry and it is negative. The July entry allowed an S3 reading - a carrier launching classes it previously could not staff - and management has now closed it themselves: the products were already on the roadmap, so AI moved the schedule, not the opportunity set.

iQIYI, Inc. Media / Streaming video · HK/CN S3 candidate (unproven)

On the Q3 2026 call (16 Nov 2026) founder and CEO Yu Gong said AI-generated content "is now the main source of supply for micro dramas and micro animations" and that "in September, they accounted for nearly 90% of the content volume and nearly 80% of total video views"; two AIGC animation titles "cut production cost by 50% to 80%", 21 revenue-sharing AIGC titles launched in Q3, and for the series Ferry Man "each chapter recouped its full production cost within the first week of release". Total revenue was down 10% year over year at RMB 6.01bn.

Mechanism
Generative video drops the marginal cost of a micro-drama or micro-animation episode by 50-80%, which lets iQIYI commission a long tail of short-form titles whose expected revenue never covered live-action production cost. Supply is then monetised through a revenue-sharing model per title rather than through the licensed long-form content budget.
Why this shape
(a) Counterfactual purchase: NOT established - viewers pay a platform subscription either way, and the company does not say any incremental subscriber or advertiser bought because of AIGC titles. (b) Prior alternative: partly established - this content volume did not exist at this cost, but live-action micro dramas do exist and Gong himself said their per-title views and revenue were "several times higher than those of AIGC titles", which points to substitution inside an existing category. (c) Capability gate: PASSED cleanly - generative video of multi-season drama quality did not exist 24 months ago. (d) Newly economic: PASSED on the company's own numbers - a 50-80% production cost cut with first-week cost recoupment is exactly the cost collapse METHOD.md treats as new-TAM-creating. Held at S3-CANDIDATE because (a) is missing and because total revenue FELL 10% year over year, so whatever AIGC is adding is not yet visible at company level.

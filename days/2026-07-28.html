The pattern, three days in: the disclosure gap is not random — it is structural. Where AI is named, no number follows; where a number is disclosed, AI is not named. Six calls this run, and not one company voluntarily bridged the two. Firms quantify what they are proud of and what investors demand; AI revenue attribution is so far in neither category. That silence is the finding, and it is repeating.

Method note for review

Neptune is the second run in a row where the most informative disclosure sits outside the schema. The appendix currently only accepts S3 with an out-of-scope subject, so an AI-native entrant posting a quantified S1 has nowhere to go and gets discarded entirely. The incumbent-vs-entrant split is one of the more interesting things this log can measure, and today it lost a clean data point to a field constraint. Worth considering whether scope: appendix should accept any shape.

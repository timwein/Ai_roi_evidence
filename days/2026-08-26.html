Woolworths Group Grocery / Retail · Australia S1 — Velocity

On the FY2026 results call (25-26 Aug 2026) CEO Amanda Bardwell said that "leveraging agentic AI, Olive, our much-loved digital shopping companion, has transformed into a personal shopping companion", with the results presentation disclosing "over 1 million unique customers who used the tool". Group eCommerce sales rose 15.9% to $10.6bn and average weekly digital visits reached 14 million, up 22.9%; neither was attributed to AI. No AI-attributed revenue, basket-size or conversion figure was given, and Bardwell said of Olive that "it is still early days".

Mechanism
Olive is a conversational assistant in the Woolworths app that Bardwell says customers use for "recipe inspiration, meal planning, and product discovery". The stated revenue path is basket construction: Smart Basket, launched in the period, "helps customers build their online baskets faster through predictive additions based on purchasing habits", which Bardwell said is "seeing positive engagement and customer retention rates" - no engagement or retention number was attached to either claim. The presentation also states "connected customers who engaged with digital platforms spent 2.4 times more than in-store-only customers in Q4", but that is a channel statistic Woolworths did not attribute to AI and is straightforwardly confounded by who shops online.
Why this shape
S1, and at the weak end of it. The only AI-specific number is adoption - unique users - not throughput, conversion or hit rate, so this sits a step below the Walmart and Albertsons disclosures already in the log, which at least offered a cohort spend differential. Revenue conversion is conspicuously absent rather than merely unstated: Woolworths sized eCommerce growth (15.9%), digital visits (+22.9%), app share of orders (>70% in Q4) and a 2.4x connected-customer spend gap on the same day, and attributed none of them to Olive, Smart Basket or any AI capability. Not S2 - nothing incremental is attributed. Not S3 - grocery baskets are the definition of pre-existing demand.

Woolworths Group Grocery / Retail · Australia C4 — Non-tech · non-engineering

In the same FY2026 results (26 Aug 2026) Bardwell said the Team Assist platform is "already managing over 7,000 queries per week and resolving nine in 10 before reaching our advisory team", and the presentation states "AI-powered catalogue planning reduced production time from a week to a few hours". No dollar saving, no headcount effect and no inference or licensing run-cost was disclosed against either.

Mechanism
Two separate non-engineering deployments at a retailer with roughly 200,000 employees. Team Assist is a retrieval-and-answer agent sitting in front of an internal advisory team, deflecting queries that would otherwise consume adviser time. The marketing assistant suite applies AI "across planning, drafting, and production" of the weekly catalogue, compressing a weekly production cycle into hours.
Why this shape
C4 - non-tech company, non-engineering function, the cell METHOD.md weights most heavily. Two standing traps apply and neither is resolved by the disclosure. Trap 1, adoption dressed as saving: 7,000 queries and a 90% deflection rate measure volume handled, not an expense line that fell. Trap 5, cost avoidance versus realized reduction: a catalogue produced in hours instead of a week frees marketing capacity, and Woolworths did not say whether that capacity was banked or reinvested. Group cost of doing business was discussed on the call but never attributed to AI. Logged in the cost ledger only - the Olive/Smart Basket revenue-side disclosure is logged separately as S1 and these are distinct claims, not one claim double-counted. Source discrepancy worth flagging: the call transcript describes Team Assist as resolving queries "before reaching our advisory team", i.e. team-facing, while the slides rendering says "resolved 9 in 10 customer interactions without human intervention". The CEO's verbatim wording is taken as authoritative; the slides rendering may be an aggregator paraphrase.

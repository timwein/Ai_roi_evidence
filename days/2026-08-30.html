Deere & Company Industrials / Agricultural machinery · US S1 — Velocity

On the Q3 FY2026 call (20 Aug 2026) Deanna Kovar said "current EOP trends suggest factory adoption of See & Spray will nearly double, with the technology included on about one-third of North American sprayers on order," and that "customers are using See & Spray on significantly more acres year over year while achieving more than 50% herbicide savings." Deere separately disclosed "more than 520 million engaged acres across nearly 1.2 million connected machines" and that "highly engaged acres have grown to more than 190 million acres, representing double-digit growth for the year." No revenue, price premium or margin contribution was attached to any of it. Production & Precision Agriculture net sales fell 6% to $3.998 billion in the quarter and are guided down about 10% for the full year.

Mechanism
See & Spray is a camera-and-machine-vision boom that identifies individual weeds in-row and fires only the nozzles above them instead of broadcasting herbicide across the field. Deere monetises it as a factory-installed option and a retrofit kit on its sprayer line, so the revenue path is a higher-ASP machine plus the Operations Center data relationship, not a per-acre fee. The disclosed 50% herbicide saving is the customer's cost saving, which is what funds the willingness to pay - it is not a Deere cost saving and is not logged as one.
Why this shape
S1 because every disclosed number is an input metric - attach rate on machines on order, acres run, engaged acres, connected machines, monthly digital users - and Deere attributed no revenue, no price premium and no margin contribution to any of them. Revenue conversion was conspicuously absent, and the direction of travel actively cuts against it: the segment that carries See & Spray shrank 6% in the quarter and is guided down about 10% for the year, so a doubling attach rate is compounding inside a contracting base. Not S3 - it fails capability gate (c), since See & Spray shipped in 2021-22 on pre-frontier computer vision, and fails prior-alternative test (b), since the farmers buying it were already buying sprayers and herbicide from Deere or a competitor. Not S2 either, because no incremental revenue was attributed to anybody. One honesty note that belongs on the record: Deere did not use the word AI about See & Spray on this call. Its only AI mention was forward-looking - Kovar saying the company "will soon build on that foundation with AI-enabled capabilities designed to unlock even more value from the data within Operations Center."

Falsifier
Attach rate only matters if it shows up in price. If FY2027 carries See & Spray to a third or more of North American sprayers on order and Deere still discloses no precision-ag price premium, mix benefit or technology-attributed revenue line, this was an adoption metric standing in for an economic one. The same conclusion follows if the attach-rate figure quietly drops out of the disclosure set. Conversely, a Q4 FY26 or FY27 call that sizes precision-ag revenue converts this entry to S2.

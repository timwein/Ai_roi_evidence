TELUS Corporation Telecom · Canada S2 — Share⚠ Counter-evidence

In its Q1 2026 results release (8 May 2026) TELUS told investors "Our AI-enabling capabilities delivered strong double-digit growth of 22 per cent" and reaffirmed "our target of circa $2 billion in 2028 across TELUS Digital and TELUS Business Solutions", citing its Rimouski Sovereign AI Factory as "sold out". Twelve weeks later, in the Q2 2026 results release (31 Jul 2026), TELUS recorded a "pre-tax, non-cash intangible asset and goodwill impairment of $2.1 billion in the second quarter, reflecting a reduction in the recoverable amount of the TELUS Digital cash-generating unit", cut FY2026 consolidated service revenue guidance from 2-4% growth to "a range of flat to negative 2 per cent", and reset the quarterly dividend by 55%. On the accompanying call CFO Gopi Chande attributed the write-down to legacy hyperscaler work being automated away faster than modelled and to AI demand arriving slower than modelled: "customer adoption is slower than we previously modeled as sales cycles have extended and deal sizes have compressed."

Mechanism
TELUS built an "AI-enabling capabilities" revenue line out of TELUS Digital (AI data annotation, complex AI datasets, CXAI) plus TELUS Business Solutions and sovereign AI compute. Two forces ran against it in the same quarter. First, the demand side arrived late: enterprise buyers of complex AI datasets lengthened sales cycles and shrank deal sizes. Second - the more interesting one - AI destroyed the incumbent revenue underneath it. Hyperscale clients automated the content moderation, ad relevancy and search-related work TELUS Digital used to perform for them, so the same technology that was supposed to open a new line closed an old one faster.
Why this shape
Counter-evidence, tagged S2 because the 22% growth and the circa-$2bn 2028 target are attributed incremental revenue on services sold into existing customer relationships, which is the shape being contradicted. Subject is in scope: TELUS Corporation is a telecom incumbent, a sector METHOD lists as In, and the disclosure is at group level. The tension is real and worth stating - TELUS Digital is itself an IT-services business, so part of this is a vendor's own sales rather than deployment ROI - but the impairment, the guidance cut and the dividend reset are group-level Tier A facts about a telecom. Note also that the sovereign AI factory line is AI-as-customer, not AI ROI on deployment, and should not be counted as either. This is the cleanest walk-back the log has yet recorded: a specific quantified AI revenue trajectory put on the record in May and impaired by `$2.1bn` in July, by the same management, eighty-four days apart.

Costco Wholesale Corporation Retail - warehouse club · US S1 — Velocity

On the Q3 FY2026 earnings call (28 May 2026) CFO Gary Millerchip said: "With consumers increasingly using AI to research products and services, we believe this has the potential to be a significant sales opportunity for Costco. We are now leveraging AI to enhance our product pages online, which in turn is increasing our relevance with the large language models." On AI-sourced traffic: "We saw triple digit growth in Q3, and this activity had the highest conversion rate of all traffic coming to our site", while volume remains low. Separately, personalized product recommendation carousels "delivered conversion rates 3x better than our typical conversion rates" and contributed just under `$0.5bn` of e-commerce sales; site and app traffic was up `37%`.

Mechanism
Two distinct channels, only one of which management labels AI. (1) Generative-search optimisation: Costco is rewriting product pages so large language models retrieve and cite them, turning LLM answer engines into an acquisition channel. The disclosed metrics are triple-digit traffic growth off a low base and the best conversion rate of any channel. (2) Personalised recommendation carousels on Costco's own surfaces, which converted at 3x the site average and carried just under `$0.5bn` of e-commerce sales in the quarter.
Why this shape
S1, not S2, and the distinction is the whole entry. Every number management attached to AI is an input metric - traffic growth, conversion multiple, traffic mix - with no revenue conversion disclosed for the AI-search channel. The one dollar figure in the quarter, the just-under-`$0.5bn` of carousel-attributed e-commerce sales, is attached to "personalized product recommendation carousels", which Millerchip did not call AI; the AI label on that number is press-applied, and treating it as AI-attributed revenue would be textbook attribution laundering. Recorded here as an input disclosure with the attribution gap stated rather than promoted to S2. Revenue conversion for the AI channel is conspicuously absent.

Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce (CIBC) Banking · Canada S1 — Velocity

At the Barclays 24th Annual Global Financial Services Conference on 15 Sep 2026, CFO Robert Sedran put the first client-facing capacity number on AdvisorAssist, the AI platform CIBC launched on 31 Jul 2026: "If an advisor could do four meetings a day with a client, now they should be able to do five" - a 25% uplift in advisor meeting capacity. Sedran described AdvisorAssist as "really is about helping advisors prepare for meetings, helping with the meeting notes and charting notes", and said the separate internal agentic system "CIBC AI internal engine that we've built ourselves is actually quite powerful and has been rolled out to the entire bank", the first such rollout "to a broad network internally" in Canadian banking. No revenue, net-new-assets, revenue-per-advisor or wealth-segment figure was attached, and the meetings number is framed as what an advisor "should be able to" do, not as measured throughput.

Mechanism
AdvisorAssist automates meeting preparation, meeting notes and charting notes for CIBC's wealth and private-banking advisors. Documentation is the binding constraint on an advisor's calendar, so removing it converts administrative hours into client-facing slots. The revenue path runs entirely through advisor capacity - one more meeting per advisor per day is roughly 20-25% more client contact, which is the input to advice sales and asset gathering. Nothing about what CIBC sells changes.
Why this shape
S1 on the METHOD test: the number is an input metric (meetings per advisor per day) on a revenue-producing process, not revenue. This is the follow-up checkpoint on the 29 Aug entry, where Culham disclosed AdvisorAssist cutting advisor administrative time "by up to 50%" with no revenue conversion. Three weeks later the bank has converted that time saving into a capacity number for the first time - which is progress along the S1 chain - but has still not converted capacity into a dollar. Revenue conversion remains conspicuously absent: no wealth-segment revenue, net new assets or revenue-per-advisor metric was offered or asked for. Status is PROJECTED, not DISCLOSED, because "should be able to do five" is management's expectation of the tool, not an observed throughput measurement; a CFO would be embarrassed to walk back a measured number and would not be embarrassed to walk back this one.

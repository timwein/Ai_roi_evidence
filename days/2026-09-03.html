Heartflow, Inc. Healthcare · US S3 candidate (unproven)

Q2 2026 total revenue of $64.1m, up 48% YoY (US revenue $59.6m, up 51%), with full-year 2026 guidance raised to $246m-$250m; management named AI Plaque Analysis - a new SKU with no predecessor in its catalog - as 'a meaningful second growth engine' but disclosed no standalone Plaque revenue figure.

Mechanism
Deep-learning quantification of coronary plaque from CCTA scans, sold as an analysis SKU distinct from the older FFRct product, and reimbursed under new 2026 Category I CPT coding for AI-enabled coronary plaque analysis (Medicare rates ~$950-$1,000). It lets cardiologists offer non-invasive plaque quantification that previously required invasive angiography or was simply not performed.
Why this shape
S3-CANDIDATE, appendix. Out of scope as subject: Heartflow is an AI-native medtech whose core product IS the AI analysis, not a non-tech incumbent deploying AI, so it is routed to the appendix as an entrant comparator per METHOD. S3 tests: (a) counterfactual - only partially met; absent AI plaque analysis some physicians would order invasive cath (a substitute, which leaks toward S2), others nothing. (b) prior alternative - no non-invasive AI plaque-quantification service existed in Heartflow's catalog; leans S3. (c) capability gate - CNN plaque quantification from CCTA is a recent capability. (d) newly economic - new 2026 CPT codes make it reimbursable, opening a service line that supported no billing before. Held at CANDIDATE because the release discloses total platform revenue but NO standalone Plaque revenue (management calls Plaque 'nascent'), so the new-TAM dollar counterfactual is not established.

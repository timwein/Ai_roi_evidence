Etsy Retail / E-commerce marketplace · US S1 — Velocity⚠ Counter-evidence

On the Q2 2026 call (6 Aug 2026) CEO Kruti Patel Goyal said: "Traffic from agentic experiences is still less than 1% of our overall traffic. We're still seeing the same things that we shared last time. They're higher intent, higher average order value traffic." No GMS, take-rate or revenue figure was attached to the channel. Logged 18 days after the call because it is the only hard ceiling any retailer has put on agentic traffic.

Mechanism
Third-party AI assistants refer shoppers into Etsy listings; Etsy's own Gifting Assistant beta offers conversational gift discovery on-platform. Both are top-of-funnel referral and discovery against the existing catalogue - no new product, no new seller supply, no new price point.
Why this shape
S1, and counter-evidence to it. The disclosed number is an input metric - share of total traffic - not revenue, which is the S1 test. Revenue conversion was conspicuously absent: management characterised the traffic as higher-intent and higher-AOV but attached no GMS or revenue figure, and said it was seeing "the same things that we shared last time", so this is a second quarter of qualitative-only characterisation. Logged as counter-evidence because it is the first named-company denominator for a channel the log's retail cluster (Walmart Sparky, Target, Lowe's Mylow, Albertsons Ask AI, Amazon Alexa for Shopping) has described only in growth rates and per-order lifts. A channel below 1% of traffic cannot yet be material to any of them, whatever its growth rate.

Ross Stores Retail (off-price) · US S1 — Velocity⚠ Counter-evidence

On the Q2 2026 call (20 Aug 2026), asked directly how AI and agentic search would affect off-price retail, CEO Jim Conroy said "AI is everywhere. No AI-attributed sales, traffic or conversion figure was offered, in a quarter when sales rose 13% to $6.26bn and comparable store sales rose 10%.

Mechanism
Conroy described AI as embedded in analytics, planning and allocation, and used daily by the company's developers, explicitly not organised as a separate function. The mechanism disclosed is internal-process, and it was explicitly not credited with the quarter's result.
Why this shape
S1, and counter-evidence to it. Nothing was quantified, so this cannot be S2 or S3; it is tagged against S1 because the analyst question was specifically about agentic search and AI-driven demand, the same thesis the log's retail S1 cluster feeds. The informative content is the refusal: a retailer posting a 10% comp whose CEO volunteers no AI attribution at all is the control case for the peers who do attribute. Not a walk-back - Ross never made a claim to walk back - which is why it is the cleaner comparator.

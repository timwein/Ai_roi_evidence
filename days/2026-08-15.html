Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison Telecom · Indonesia S2 — Share

In its H1 2026 results release (29 Jul 2026) Indosat reported revenue of IDR30.7 trillion, up 13.1% YoY, and attributed AI-powered customer experiences directly to a revenue-per-user outcome: "These differentiated experiences helped drive a 19.9% YoY increase in data traffic and a 17.3% YoY increase in Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)", to IDR46 thousand. The named experiences are the Anti-Spam and Anti-Scam feature, Sahabat-AI, Gemini AI and Adobe Express. Mobile subscribers were flat at 93.4 million, so the ARPU gain is monetisation of an existing base rather than customer acquisition.

Mechanism
Two mechanisms are mixed and the company does not split them. (1) AI-driven hyperpersonalisation of offers and pricing against a 93.4m-subscriber base - genuine deployment, and the reading that would make this a real S2. (2) Bundling third-party AI subscriptions (Gemini, Adobe Express) into mobile plans as paid add-ons - that is distribution of somebody else's AI, closer to a reseller margin than to ROI on Indosat's own AI spend. The disclosed ARPU number covers both.
Why this shape
S2, not S3. Test (a): absent the AI, these subscribers were already buying mobile data from Indosat or a rival - the counterfactual purchase plainly exists. Test (b): the prior alternative was a cheaper plan from the same carrier or from Telkomsel/XLSmart, so any share gain came out of a competitor or out of the customer's own downgrade decision. Zero-sum at the Indonesian industry level and GDP-neutral: this is monetisation intensity on a flat subscriber base, not new demand. Worth logging anyway because a non-tech incumbent putting a specific revenue-per-user figure next to AI on the record is rare - most of this log's S1/S2 entries stop at input metrics.

OpenAI / Chatterji, Holtz, Rakholia, Tambe & Weeratunga - 'How Organizations Use AI: Evidence from ChatGPT' (1,500+ organizations, 17m+ messages, 417 US public-company tickers) Cross-sector (research) · US S2 — Share⚠ Counter-evidence

Working paper submitted 12 Aug 2026 linking ChatGPT Enterprise account records to Compustat financials for US public companies through March 2026. On the only revenue question it asks, the answer is null: "Revenue per employee is not meaningfully associated with output tokens per employee once other controls are included", and for the other usage margins "the point estimates are positive for messages per active usage week per employee and weekly active users per employee, but neither coefficient is statistically significant." Adoption runs the other way - "firms with higher revenue per employee are more likely to adopt ChatGPT Enterprise", with "a one-log-point increase in lagged revenue per employee ... associated with roughly 0.4 to 0.9 percentage points higher adoption probability."

Mechanism
Not a deployment finding - a measurement of whether the revenue signal this log hunts one company at a time shows up in the largest linked enterprise-AI usage dataset that exists. 521 ticker-year observations across 417 tickers with positive message volume, fiscal years 2024-2025.
Why this shape
Counter-evidence tagged to S2, the shape that claims AI produces attributable incremental revenue at adopting firms. Read it precisely, because the headline overstates it: revenue per employee here is LAGGED - measured before adoption - so this is a cross-sectional selection regression, not a causal test of whether AI raised anyone's revenue. What it establishes is (i) high-revenue-per-employee firms adopt more, i.e. adoption is selected on prior performance, which is exactly the endogeneity that makes every adopter-outperforms-non-adopter statistic worthless, and (ii) among adopters, usage intensity carries no measurable revenue signal. The load-bearing fact is the absence: OpenAI had ChatGPT Enterprise usage linked to Compustat financials through March 2026 - the data needed to test post-adoption revenue changes - and published 69 pages of adoption, seniority and task taxonomy without that result. The strongest-incentive party with the best dataset did not produce the number.

Williams-Sonoma, Inc. Retail · US S1 — Velocity

On the Q2 FY2026 call (26 Aug 2026) Chief Technology and Digital Officer Sameer Hassan said of the Williams Sonoma brand's AI shopping assistant: "Engagement with Olive is up 700%. Revenue is up 620%, and customers who engage with Olive convert at 3 times a higher rate", and that "A visit where we personalize the experience now generates roughly 9 times the revenue of an average visit. Last year, that was 2X." A second rendering of the same transcript dates both Olive figures "since the beginning of the year". Total Q2 revenue was $1.96bn, +6.7%, with comparable brand revenue +6.2%; none of it was attributed to AI and no incremental dollars were sized. CEO Laura Alber's only quantified framing was qualitative - "AI is an accelerator to our strategy and our productivity."

Mechanism
Olive is a conversational shopping assistant on the Williams Sonoma brand site that takes open-ended intents and returns product sets; the disclosed revenue path is conversion, with engagers converting at 3x the site rate. Separately, an e-commerce personalization layer selects on-site content per visit, and Williams-Sonoma measures a personalized visit against an average visit. The 9x figure is a ratio between two visit populations, not a lift measured against a held-out control.
Why this shape
S1, not S2, because the load-bearing numbers are input metrics - engagement, a conversion multiple, and a revenue-per-visit ratio. "Revenue is up 620%" is growth in revenue transacted through the Olive surface off an undisclosed base, which is the denominator game METHOD names: a numerator with no base is not attributed incremental revenue and cannot promote to S2. Revenue conversion was conspicuously absent: no dollars, no share of e-commerce, no statement that any of the 6.2% comp was Olive-driven. The 9x personalized-visit figure is confounded by self-selection - high-intent visitors are more likely both to be personalized to and to buy - and Williams-Sonoma offered no counterfactual.

Williams-Sonoma, Inc. Retail · US C4 — Non-tech · non-engineering

On the same Q2 FY2026 call (26 Aug 2026) Sameer Hassan said of Otto, the assistant launched for the Pottery Barn brands: "Over 70% of engagements with Otto are able to be resolved without handing you over to a person." No dollar saving, no headcount action, no service-cost-per-contact figure was attached, and Williams-Sonoma did not say what the pre-Otto deflection rate was.

Mechanism
A customer-service assistant fronting the Pottery Barn brands' contact channels, resolving order, product and post-purchase queries without escalation to a human agent. The cost path is contact deflection - each resolved engagement is an agent-handled contact not incurred - but the company disclosed the deflection rate, not the contact cost or the volume it applies to.
Why this shape
C4 - non-tech company, non-engineering function. Logged on the same footing as Woolworths Team Assist (26 Aug 2026): a specific, quantified deflection rate from a Tier A source with no dollar attached. METHOD trap 5 applies in advance: even at face value this is cost avoidance on contacts, not a reduction in a disclosed expense line, and without a baseline deflection rate the 70% cannot be read as incremental. Logged separately from the Olive S1 entry because it is a distinct disclosure about a distinct product, not a second reading of the same number.

ING Groep N.V. Banking · Netherlands S1 — Velocity

At the Bank of America 31st Annual Financials CEO Conference on 23 Sep 2026, CEO Steven van Rijswijk put the first timings on ING's agentic mortgage assistant in the Netherlands: a simple mortgage now runs "from requirement to approval in 30 minutes", and complex cases have gone "from seven days to five days using agentic AI". He also disclosed that chatbot traffic routed through AI has risen from 45% to 75% over the past couple of years, that 82% of customer journeys are now straight-through digital with no manual intervention, and that ING employs 600 AI professionals including 300 in a hub in Turkey. At the same event ING added EUR 1 billion to revenue guidance, took 2027 revenue above EUR 26 billion and said fee income had already hit the EUR 5 billion target set for 2027 - and attributed none of those three numbers to AI.

Mechanism
An agentic assistant reads a mortgage file, resolves document verification and internal handovers, explains the likely outcome and proposes next steps; an ING employee still makes and owns every approval decision. The revenue path is origination capacity: a fixed pool of mortgage specialists (about 50 were on the tool when ING described the rollout in June) clears more files per day, and in a Dutch market where lenders compete on turnaround, a faster binding decision wins business from brokers who route to whoever answers first.
Why this shape
S1 because every disclosed number is an input metric - approval time, chatbot routing share, straight-through journey share, AI headcount - and not one euro of revenue is attributed. The absence is unusually loud here. Van Rijswijk raised revenue guidance by EUR 1 billion and reported the 2027 fee-income target reached a year early in the same appearance, and did not connect either to the AI capability he had just spent minutes describing. Either the mortgage agent is not yet material to the top line, or ING is unwilling to carve it out; the disclosure does not let you tell which, and that is the whole S1 caveat in one event.

TD Bank Group Banking · Canada S1 — Velocity

At Scotiabank's 27th Annual Financials Summit on 9 Sep 2026 - two weeks before this run and previously unlogged - CEO Ray Chun put process-level numbers behind TD's AI programme for the first time. Mortgage pre-adjudication has been taken from a "15-hour process... down to on average, three minutes". Unit costs in the same chain: "Mortgage funding on average costs us about CAD 187. It's going down to CAD 47, CAD 46. Discharges are going from CAD 22, CAD 23 down to about CAD 11." On collections: "When we were using people, the connect rate was about 6%, 7%. Our connect rate using our agents now with the AI behind it and getting to them at the right times, is between 20%-25%." Chun named five enterprise priorities - "Contact center is one. End to end retail credit, fraud, AML, KYC, and advisor productivity" - and said of agentic AI that "you can finally truly go end to end and reimagine entire processes".

Mechanism
Agentic AI rebuilt rather than accelerated the retail credit chain. Pre-adjudication - pulling documents, checking income and covenants, forming a preliminary credit view - was 15 hours of human work per file and is now a three-minute machine pass with a human decision on top, so origination capacity per underwriter rises by roughly two orders of magnitude on that step. In collections, AI selects contact timing and channel, quadrupling the share of outreach that reaches a live borrower, which pulls recoveries forward.
Why this shape
S1, and logged in the revenue ledger rather than as C4 under METHOD's one-disclosure-one-ledger rule, for the same reason as the Ategrity precedent: what this disclosure is about is capacity in a revenue-producing process, not an expense line that fell. The CAD 187-to-CAD 46 funding cost and the CAD 22-to-CAD 11 discharge cost are recorded here as mechanism and are deliberately not logged again as cost entries. The revenue conversion is still missing: TD's own medium-term target is explicitly half revenue uplift and half cost savings, and Chun sized neither half at this event.

Astrana Health, Inc. Healthcare providers · US C4 — Non-tech · non-engineering

In an investor presentation filed as Exhibit 99.1 to a Form 8-K on 23 Sep 2026, Astrana Health states under Operating Leverage: "AI-native operating system has led to 210 bps G&A improvement year over year (5.6% in Q2 2026, 7.7% in Q2 2025)". The same deck attaches operating numbers to the platform: a "30% efficiency gain" defined as reduction in admin time per care manager, "~70%" of all prior-authorisation requests auto-approved, under two minutes decision time on auto-adjudicated requests, and 500,000 agentic patient interactions per month by voice and SMS. FY2026 revenue guidance is USD 3.8-4.1 billion, so 210bps of G&A is roughly USD 80-86 million annualised against that base.

Mechanism
Astrana runs a unified data layer under an agent tier that does prior authorisation, claims adjudication and audit, referral management and outbound patient engagement. Work that a delegated risk-bearing organisation would staff with review nurses, authorisation clerks and outreach callers is executed by agents with humans on exception, which is what shows up as G&A falling as a share of revenue while revenue grows.
Why this shape
C4 - non-tech sector, non-engineering function - which is the cell METHOD weights most heavily, and this is a rare instance of it with a filed number rather than a survey percentage. Standing trap #4 applies, headcount and cost with many parents: Astrana closed the Prospect acquisition and is separately guiding to the high end of a USD 12-15 million synergy range in the same bullet block, so ordinary post-deal scale and the AI platform are both pushing the same ratio down and the deck does not decompose them. Trap #3 does not apply: this is a defined GAAP G&A ratio, not a composite 'value' construct. The ~70% prior-auth auto-approval is described in the deck as "driving increased patient access", which is the nearest thing to a revenue path and is not quantified.

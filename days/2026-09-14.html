BJ's Wholesale Club Holdings and Philippine Airlines (AI-agent deployments disclosed by ibex Ltd) Retail (warehouse club) / Airlines · US / Philippines C4 — Non-tech · non-engineering

On the ibex Ltd Q4 FY2026 earnings call (10 Sep 2026) CEO Bob Dechant said the AI agent built for Philippine Airlines, an existing client, "launched an AI agent solution in three languages, English, Tagalog, and Taglish, achieved resolution rates above 20%, and delivered CSAT above 4.7 out of 5.0", and that BJ's Wholesale was "a new trophy client win in which we led with our AI solution, not traditional BPO", which "launched in June and achieved impressive results in weeks, not months", "attaining resolution rates above 40% and CSAT scores above 4.7 out of 5.0". ibex said it has "double digit client deployments with our AI agent solution spread across five verticals" and disclosed no revenue, pricing or cost figure for the AI line; no analyst asked it to.

Mechanism
Conversational AI agents, built on ibex's Sierra partnership, take inbound customer contacts end to end and close them without a human handoff. The resolution rate is the share of contacts the agent finishes alone; every resolved contact is a seat-minute the deploying company does not buy. At BJ's the AI agent was the entry product rather than an add-on to an existing human-seat contract, which is why the 40% figure is measured from launch rather than against a prior human baseline.
Why this shape
C4: the deploying companies are a warehouse-club retailer and a flag airline - non-tech - and the function is customer service, not software engineering. Kept out of the revenue ledger because a resolution rate is contact deflection with no disclosed revenue path; the CSAT figure is a quality guardrail, not a revenue metric. Trap 5 applies squarely: a resolution rate is not an expense line that fell, and neither deployer has published a service-cost number to test it against. The load-bearing weakness is the source, not the tier - this is a Tier A call, but it is the outsourcer's call, and neither BJ's nor Philippine Airlines has confirmed any of it. Trap 1 does not apply: resolution rate is an outcome, not an adoption count.

Insilico Medicine Biotechnology / drug discovery · HK/CN S2 — Share

In interim results announced 26 Aug 2026, Insilico Medicine reported H1 2026 revenue of US$106.3 million, up 287.2% year on year, at a 90.3% gross margin, with net profit of US$35.54 million (adjusted non-IFRS US$51.23 million) - its first profitable interim period. US$103.1 million of that (97% of revenue) came from drug discovery and pipeline development, which the company attributed to "large-amount upfront payments from a series of key business development (BD) deals reached during the period, as well as milestone payments continually confirmed from existing collaborations" - named partners include Eli Lilly, Servier, SK Biopharmaceuticals and Takeda. Software solutions revenue was US$2.70 million, 2.5% of the total.

Mechanism
Generative-chemistry and target-discovery models produce preclinical assets in-house; the assets are then out-licensed to pharma partners for upfront and milestone payments. The revenue is the sale of AI-originated molecules, not the sale of AI software - software solutions are only 2.5% of the half. This is the cleanest available read on whether an AI-originated product actually converts into cash from third-party buyers.
Why this shape
S2, not S3, and the distinction is the whole point. (a) Counterfactual purchase: Eli Lilly, Servier, SK Biopharmaceuticals and Takeda in-license preclinical assets continuously; absent Insilico they would have bought something. (b) Prior alternative: conventional discovery biotechs and the partners' own internal programmes - a competitor lost the deal. (c) Capability gate: Insilico's platform is generative chemistry and multi-omics target ranking that predates the last ~24 months; no recent frontier jump is load-bearing. (d) Newly economic: partially - discovery cost per asset falls - but the buyer set is unchanged, so this reallocates pharma BD spend rather than creating new demand. Appendix scope because the subject is an AI-native drug-discovery company, not a deploying incumbent; it is logged because METHOD's appendix exists precisely so entrant evidence stays visible next to the incumbent log.

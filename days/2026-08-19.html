Rakuten Group E-commerce / Internet services · Japan S2 — Share

In its Q2 FY2026 results release (10 Aug 2026) Rakuten disclosed that "the advertising business achieved high growth with revenue reaching 65.6 billion yen, up 15.6% YoY, driven by AI-powered search and targeted banner advertising on Rakuten Ichiba and Rakuten Travel". Group revenue was 665.5bn yen (+11.6%) and non-GAAP operating income 42.0bn yen (+109.6%). No AI-specific share of the 15.6% was given.

Mechanism
AI applied to ad search relevance and banner targeting across the Ichiba marketplace and Rakuten Travel, with AI also generating ad creative and running screening and bidding across large campaign volumes. Trade coverage (MediaPost, 10 Aug) adds that Rakuten's conversational chatbot shortened time from item discovery to checkout and raised average order value on Ichiba, with no figure disclosed. The same release notes that "from Q1/26, a portion of AI-related development costs are allocated to each business" - the run-cost offset is being pushed into segment P&L.
Why this shape
S2, and the losers are rival Japanese ad platforms and retail-media networks - these advertisers were already buying inventory somewhere. Fails S3 test (a): the counterfactual purchase clearly exists. Fails (c): search relevance ranking and automated bidding are not a capability that appeared in the last 24 months. The entry's real weakness is undecomposed attribution: the whole 15.6% is narrated as AI-driven with no split against Ichiba GMV growth or ad-load, and no counterfactual offered. Subject scope: Rakuten is an e-commerce, fintech and mobile conglomerate, not a software vendor, hyperscaler or AI-native, so main rather than appendix - the same treatment given to Booking and Expedia in this log.

Target Corporation Retail · US S1 — Velocity

On the Q2 2026 earnings call (19 Aug 2026) CMO Cara Sylvester said AI-powered Teacher and College Wish List recommendations and more personalized app content produced "total wish list creations up more than 50% to last year, items added to these lists more than doubling, and conversion across our key back-to-school pages up nearly 20%". Q2 digital comparable sales were +8.7% and total comps +3.8%, neither attributed to AI, and no incremental revenue dollars were sized.

Mechanism
AI-generated wish-list recommendations for teacher and college registries plus personalised content on the app home screen, applied to back-to-school category pages. Separately CEO Michael Fiddelke said Target's "digital traffic sourced from external AI platforms is growing more than 3.5 times the industry as compared to a year ago" off partnerships with OpenAI and Google Gemini, and named Chandhu Nair as a new Chief AI Officer. CFO Jim Lee disclosed Roundel gross billings +nearly 20%, Target Plus marketplace GMV +more than 40% and Circle 360 membership revenue +over 40% - none of which Target attributed to AI.
Why this shape
S1: the AI-attributed number is conversion, an input metric, not revenue. Revenue conversion is conspicuously absent rather than merely unstated - digital comps (+8.7%) and three separate high-growth non-merchandise lines were disclosed on the same call and none was tied to the AI capability. The conversion figure is scoped to "key back-to-school pages", a seasonal category subset, with no baseline conversion rate and no counterfactual for a quarter in which traffic rose 3.6% anyway.
